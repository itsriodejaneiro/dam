# Sign in

Visitors read the map without an account. Editing starts at **sign in**, in the header, which opens `/login`.

![The sign-in page](/screens/sign-in-1.png)

Enter the email and password of your [account](./accounts.md). The superuser of the deployment signs in on the same page. You arrive at [Entities](./entities.md), or at the page that asked you to sign in.

**log out**, in the header, ends the session, after a confirmation.

Accounts have no public sign-up: someone with an account [creates yours](./accounts.md).
