# Journeys for visitors

A journey tells a process step by step over the map: as you read, the map moves to what each step is about. **journeys**, in the header, opens the list at `/journeys`, with the phases and steps of each one.

![The list of journeys](/screens/journeys-1.png)

Open a journey to read it at `/journeys/<slug>`. Only published journeys appear, and a deployment can [hide the section](./configure.md#the-fields).

![The opening of a journey](/screens/journeys-2.png)

## Read a journey

Scroll, and the page follows you. On a wide window it has three columns:

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **left** | the timeline: the phases and steps, how far you have read, and a click to jump to any of them |
| **middle** | the map, framed on what the step involves, with the rest faded. Above it, the documents |
| **right** | the steps, one after the other |

![A step of a journey, with the map framed on the entities it involves](/screens/journeys-3.png)

On a narrower window the timeline becomes a bar across the top, and on a phone a counter with the progress.

## Documents

Many processes hand a person papers along the way. The **Documents** strip above the map shows what you hold so far, and it grows as you read.

## The end of a phase

A phase can close with a card: what it achieved, the documents obtained during it and the documents needed to go on.

![The card that closes a phase](/screens/journeys-4.png)

## Read about an entity

Click a highlighted entity on the map to read its full name, category and description without leaving the journey.

![An entity opened from a journey](/screens/journeys-5.png)
